About

I'm a software engineer with 5+ years of experience, currently an AI Engineer at BluGrass Technologies in Dubai. I started on mobile: I led a Flutter HR system covering attendance, payroll and leave management, used by 1,000+ monthly active users on Android and iOS.

Today I build LLM features, AI agents with function calling, and chatbots for business products, and I benchmark OpenAI, Claude and Llama models to pick the right one for each use case on accuracy, speed and cost.

I test everything I deliver: user flows, APIs, login and permissions, edge cases and AI outputs. When something breaks, I trace it to the root cause and run regression tests after the fix. I use AI coding tools, and I review and test all of their output before release.
