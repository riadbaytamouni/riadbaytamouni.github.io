About

I'm a software engineer with 5+ years of experience, now an AI Engineer at BluGrass Technologies in Dubai. I started on mobile, leading a Flutter HR system for attendance, payroll and leave management used by 1,000+ monthly active users on Android and iOS.

Today I build LLM features, AI agents with function calling, and chatbots for business products. I benchmark OpenAI, Claude and Llama models to choose the right one for each use case on accuracy, speed and cost.
